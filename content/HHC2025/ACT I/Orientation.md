## Challenge Description

```
Meet Lynn Schifano on the train for a warm welcome and get ready for your journey around the Dosis Neighborhood.
```

## Solution

This year's Holiday Hack Challenge starts on a train heading to **The Neighborhood**. Your first task is to talk to [[Lynn Schifano]] who will guide you trough your first terminal.

**Note**: The ambient sound in the train is a highly recommended download to use as your mobile phone ringtone: [ambience-train.mp3](https://2025.holidayhackchallenge.com/sfx%2Fambiance-train.mp3).

Click on the terminal called **"First Terminal"**. You will be provided with instructions to type `answer` in the top pane:

![[Orientation.png]]

Once completed, make sure to talk to Lynn to receive your badge, learn about the new map rotation by pressing `Q` and `E`, and be able to leave the train and begin your journey around The Neighborhood.
